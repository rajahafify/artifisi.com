The idea

Make airasia the place Asia goes for a daily game.

One games channel inside the app, with a shelf that grows every season. Like Netflix, for short games about the places you could fly to next.

The daily game · play it here

Guess Where

One destination a day, told one clue at a time. Guess the city.

  • Six clues, from food and festivals down to the airport code.
  • Every wrong guess shows how far off you are, which way to go, and if the country is right.
  • Ends with a short note on the place and a button to flights there.
  • A spoiler-free result to share on WhatsApp, with the airasia mark.

Guess Where · daily and ranked

Three puzzles a day, one board for Asia

Easy

A famous place

Keeps the daily habit open to everyone.

Medium

A known name

A city people have heard of but may not place.

Hard

Neighbours

Close cities that are easy to mix up. Counts the most.

  • Solve on the first clue for 6 points, on the last for 1. Medium counts double, Hard triple.
  • Boards for today, this week and this month, by country and with friends.
  • Streaks and top ranks can pay out in airasia points, if airasia wants that.
  • First name only, with an option to hide. Answers are checked on the server.
