Four exits. The four sales side by side, coin area to scale. CyberArk went to Palo Alto Networks for US$25B (closed Feb 2026). Waze went to Google in 2013, Moovit to Intel's Mobileye in 2020 for US$900M, and Viber to Rakuten in 2014 for US$900M.

The numbers. Two days, eleven products, four open categories.

The week. 20 Oct: the National Cyber Security Committee, chaired by the PM, decides on Waze. 21 Oct: the PMO confirms an immediate public sector ban and the PM urges people to move to homegrown apps like TuJu. 22 Oct: TuJu buckles under demand. 23 Oct: the PM says a nationwide ban needs careful study first, and Waze calls the data security claims completely false. TuJu was built by the Malaysian Highway Authority (LLM) with Prosignal Consortium and launched on 26 Nov 2025.

The ask

A ban creates demand. Malaysian firms should be ready to meet it.

Buy local first

Make government agencies the first customer for the four ready categories, starting with TuJu capacity.

Fund the gaps

Target grants and procurement at messaging, work tools and security products, where no local player exists.

Publish the test

A clear, published data-sovereignty standard tells local firms exactly what they need to build to.

Three asks. Three asks that run together.

What each covers. Buy local first covers the four ready categories. Fund the gaps names messaging, work tools and security products. Publish the test applies to all eight.

Methodology

Scope. Consumer and business software categories where a widely used product is incorporated in Israel or was founded there. "11 Israeli-incorporated products" counts companies meeting the government's Waze test in our full ledger. Surveillance tools sold only to governments are left out.

Readiness. Ready: a Malaysian product competes today. Open gap: no significant Malaysian player. Ratings are our judgement from public information, not a product audit.

Limits. Shows corporate links only; it does not claim any product leaks Malaysian user data. Acquisition prices are announced headline values. Figures as of 24 Oct 2026; ownership changes often.
