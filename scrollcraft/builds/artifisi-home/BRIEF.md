# Artifisi home page: brief

Authored from fify's answers in the website thread (29 and 30 Oct 2026), not from a fresh eight-question interview. Where fify did not answer a topic, the decision is marked **Authored**.

## 1. Vibe
fify: "think awward wining". Earlier: "i hate it", "the whole below the fold feels unstructured", "this feels really bad", "still too basic".
Authored: confident, technical, two-sided, dark. References: a split-screen film title sequence, a game's character select screen.

## 2. The journey (fify's words)
"artifisi.com should be about the company. mision vision objective, who we are what we do. move all the dunia stuff to artifisi.com/projects/dunia. keep a simple, what is dunia, show the video, some summary, link to the dunia page. what game do we build, some summary, and maybe link to our games page."
"dont hide our video. i want it to be front and center."
Keep the hero and its line: "People play games. We turn what they do into training data for AI."

## 3. Energy curve
Authored: loud hero (the existing 3D scene), quieter statements, building through the games, loudest at the Dunia film, calm at the founder, firm at contact.

## 4. Feeling, stage by stage, and the one moment
Authored. The one moment: the divider sweeps away and the Dunia film takes the whole screen.

## 5. Something no other site does
Authored: the page is split down the middle, games on the left and AI on the right, and pieces of gameplay cross the divider and land as rows of training data.

## 6. Distance from premium-minimal
Authored: dense-technical, not minimal. Two grounds (teal for games, blue for AI), IBM Plex, big type.

## 7. One world or distinct scenes
Authored: one split held for the whole page, resolved at the film. Not a continuous camera flight.

## 8. Assets
Real: the Dunia demo video (Google Drive embed, 34.8 MB, cannot be downloaded into the build), screenshots of Melaka 1511, Orbwalker (four) and Hullpack Hero, the three.js hero. No generation key, so nothing is generated.

## What the visitor must believe by the end
Artifisi makes real games, and those games are how it builds data for AI.

## The one action
Write to us (the contact form, label "Write to us" everywhere).

## Grammar
Split stage. The company is literally two divisions and the hero already splits the screen into Games and AI, so the format is the argument. Why the others lost: filmic one-shot needs scrub footage we do not have; continuous world needs a real geography; typographic poster wastes the screenshots and the film; gallery makes Dunia a caption; live surface would need a fake dashboard; chaptered editorial is what fify already rejected as "unstructured"; cutlist is the wrong energy for a company page.

## Feeling curve
| Act | Feeling | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | curiosity | the 3D city and the data cube, split in two |
| 1 Who we are | recognition | one half says "We make games", the other "We build AI", and the halves finish each other's sentence |

Peak: "it's the site where the screen splits in half and then the video takes over." Act 4, largest span on the page. Act 3 before it is busy; the silence before the peak is the last row landing and the divider pausing.

Tell-someone sentence: "It's the site where you watch gameplay turn into training data as you scroll."

## Signature move
**Gameplay crosses the divider.** Small squares leave each game's screenshot on the left, cross the divider and become rows in a labelled data sheet on the right. The divider counts the rows that have crossed (a count of rows on the page, not a business figure). The rows are sample data and say so.
