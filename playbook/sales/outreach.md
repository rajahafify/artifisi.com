## Rules

- Send from `hafify@artifisi.com`. Plain text, no attachments, no signature image.
- Two emails maximum. If there is no reply after the follow-up, stop.
- Log the date, the recipient and the reply in `company-updates/docs/brainstorm.html`.
- Never send a sample to an organisation without emailing them first. The noindex tag protects the search ranking, not the relationship.
