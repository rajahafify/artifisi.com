## What we sell

We turn a published report into an interactive web page and a set of vertical videos, from the PDF, in under a week.

The page keeps every number as real text, so search engines and screen readers can read it. The charts are drawn in three.js and re-form as the reader scrolls. The videos run 40 to 55 seconds, captioned, with a voice-over, sized for LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok.

## Why this exists

Your report is 90 pages. Most of the people you want to reach will read the executive summary, if that. A page and three shorts give the same numbers a second life, and give your comms team something to post for a month.

## What we need from you

- The final PDF, and the spreadsheet behind it if one exists
- Permission to reproduce the report's own charts and images
- One person who can approve numbers within two days
- Logo files and brand colours, if you want them used

## What we do not do

- We do not check your numbers. We copy them exactly as the report prints them, and you approve every figure before we publish.
- We do not write the report, the analysis or the conclusions.
- We do not host anything permanently. The page is static and can sit on your domain or ours.

## Timing
