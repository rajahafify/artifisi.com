## 1. What this covers

We will produce the deliverables listed in the Schedule. Anything not in the Schedule is out of scope and quoted separately.

## 2. What you give us

(a) the final source report as a PDF, and any data files behind it;
(b) written confirmation that you have the right to let us reproduce its text, numbers, charts and images;
(c) one named approver, who returns comments on each draft within two working days.

## 3. Numbers

We copy figures from the source report exactly as printed. We do not audit, verify or recalculate them. You approve every figure before publication. If a figure is wrong in the source, it will be wrong on the page, and that is not a breach of this agreement.

## 4. Delivery and approval

We deliver a first draft by the date in the Schedule. Two rounds of changes are included. A round means one consolidated set of comments from the named approver. Further rounds are billed at RM250 each. We publish only after you approve in writing.

## 5. Fee and payment

The fee is stated in the Schedule. 50% on signature, 50% on delivery. Invoices are payable within 14 days. Overdue amounts carry interest at 1.5% a month. Service tax is charged where the law requires it.

## 6. If the work stops

If you cancel after we start, the deposit is not refunded and we invoice for work done to that point. If we cannot finish for reasons within our control, we refund the fee for the part we did not deliver.

## 7. Ownership

On full payment you own the page and the videos, and may host, edit and republish them. We keep ownership of our engine, chart code, templates and tooling, and of anything we made before this agreement. We may show the work in our portfolio and on our site unless you tell us in writing not to.

## 8. Attribution and endorsement

Until you approve the page, it carries a line saying it is unofficial and was not made by or for you. We will not use your name or logo in marketing without your written consent. Nothing here makes either party the agent of the other, or implies your endorsement of our other products.

## 9. Confidentiality

We keep unpublished material confidential and use it only for this work. We delete our copy within 30 days of delivery if you ask.

## 10. Liability

Our total liability under this agreement is limited to the fee you paid us. We are not liable for decisions you or anyone else makes using the report or the page.

## 11. Term and law

This agreement starts on signature and ends when the deliverables are accepted and paid for. Malaysian law applies and the courts of Kuala Lumpur have jurisdiction.
