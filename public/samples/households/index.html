The squeeze

The poorest tenth now keeps almost nothing back

In 2019, households in the bottom 10% had 10.8% of their income left after spending. In 2024 it was 1.9%. Their gross income in 2024 was RM2,227 a month.

Top 10%Rise in monthly spending, 2019 to 2024RM65

For the bottom 10% that rise was 21.3%. Spending for every group in the lower 70% rose by more than 15%.

Left over. Two jars of bottom 10% income. The bright cubes at the top are what is left after spending: 10.8% in 2019, 1.9% in 2024.

Spending up. How much more a month each group spent in 2024 than in 2019. RM384 more for the bottom 10%, RM65 more for the top 10%.

Ageing

Perak is getting old fastest

In 2024, 41.7% of households in Perak had at least one older person living in them. Two years earlier it was 35.5%.

The fine print

Where this comes from

Also in the report

01

Services nearby

97.8% of households live within 5km of a public health facility, 98.7% within 5km of a primary school and 95.9% within 5km of a secondary school. Treated water, electricity and telecoms are close to universal.

02

Work

Labour force participation was 70.6%. Formal jobs sit mostly in cities. Skill-related underemployment was 18.6% in Kuala Lumpur and 26.5% in Pulau Pinang, the two lowest reported. Women take part less and earn less than men.

03

Food supply

Malaysia buys from a few import sources. Nearly three quarters of beef imports come from India and about two thirds of vegetable imports come from China.
