The gap

People come to ikhlas.com for big moments. Then the app stays closed.

Umrah

Zakat

Between those moments there is nothing to open the app for, so the brand has to buy attention again every season.

The idea

Make ikhlas.com the place Muslim families go for games.

One games channel inside the app, with a shelf that grows every season. Like Netflix, for games parents trust and every age can play.

The daily game · play it here

Teka Surah

One story a day, told one hint at a time. Guess which surah it comes from.

  • Six hints, each an event from the story, never a quote.
  • Every wrong guess shows where the surah sits, where it was revealed, and how long it is.
  • Ends with a short summary and the verse references, checked by your ustaz.
  • A spoiler-free result to share on WhatsApp, with the ikhlas.com mark.

Bazaar Basra

Basra in the time of Umar. Buy stock on the weekend, run your stall through the week, and at the end of the year count what you own and pay zakat.
