# Brief

Stage 1 of `playbook/pipeline.md`. Reconstructed on 2026-10-26 from `source.md`, `story-v2.md`
and `sections.md`, after the report was built. It was skipped the first time round. Reconstructing
it does not make it the thing that shaped the report, and the log says so.

**Topic**: What Malaysia pays, by industry, 2010 to 2025. The gap between the best-paid and the
worst-paid industry, and the fact that the ladder narrowed for a decade, broke in 2020, and never
got back to 2019.

**Audience**: Malaysian employers, hiring managers, and anyone benchmarking a salary offer. The
secondary audience is Malaysian readers who want the national wage data on a phone, without a PDF.

**Question they arrive with**: what does a job actually pay here, compared to everyone else? If
you are making an offer, the version of that question is: is this still competitive, and does the
industry I am hiring from matter as much as it used to?

**Why now**: the DOSM Salaries and Wages Survey publishes annually, and 2025 is the most recent
complete year. The full 2010 to 2025 series had not been pulled before. The earlier draft used
two snapshots, 2010 and 2025, which was enough to produce a claim the full series did not
support.

**What people search for**: "malaysia median salary", "salary by industry malaysia",
"DOSM wages survey", "malaysian wages 2025", "wage gap malaysia". Every figure on the page is
plain text, so the page answers the search that sent someone to it.

**Pass**: the audience is named, and the question they arrive with is named.
