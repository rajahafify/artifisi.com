The problem

A report is a bad shape for the internet.

Most of the people you want to reach will read the executive summary, if that. The numbers are good. They are just locked in a file nobody opens on a phone.

Your comms team still has a month of posts to fill, and a graph from page 42 is not a post.

What you get

Three things, from the same numbers.

01

The page

Every figure is real text, so search engines and screen readers can read it. The charts are drawn in 3D and re-form as the reader scrolls. Tap one to switch view. On a phone it becomes a text page, then a full screen chart.

02

The videos

Three vertical films, 40 to 55 seconds, captioned, with a voice over. Sized for LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok. Each one covers a single number, so they can run in any order.

03

The stills

Every chart as a press ready image, so the report page, the deck and the launch post all match. No one has to screenshot a PDF again.

The fine print, early

What we do not do.

We do not check your numbers

We copy figures exactly as the report prints them. You approve every one before we publish. If a number is wrong in the source, it will be wrong on the page.

We do not claim your work

Until you approve it, the page says it is unofficial and was not made by or for you. We do not use your name or logo without written consent.

We do not keep your page

You own the page and the videos once the invoice is paid. We keep the engine and the chart code, and we may show the work in our portfolio.

We do not overrun quietly

If we miss a date we agreed, the fee drops 10% for each working day we are late. If we cannot finish, you get back the part we did not deliver.
