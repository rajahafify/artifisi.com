The fine print, early

What we do not do.

We do not check your numbers

We copy figures exactly as the report prints them. You approve every one before we publish. If a number is wrong in the source, it will be wrong on the page.

We do not claim your work

Until you approve it, the page says it is unofficial and was not made by or for you. We do not use your name or logo without written consent.

We do not keep your page

You own the page and the videos once the invoice is paid. We keep the engine and the chart code, and we may show the work in our portfolio.

We do not overrun quietly

If we miss a date we agreed, the fee drops 10% for each working day we are late. If we cannot finish, you get back the part we did not deliver.
