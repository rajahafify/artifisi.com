Consumer prices

Inflation eased. Fuel did not.

Department of Statistics Malaysia
Consumer Price Index, September 2026

Malaysia's consumer price index rose 1.8% in the year to September 2026. Inside that number, transport heated to 2.5% while housing cooled to 1.4%, the market price of RON95 averaged RM4.18, and the cheapest state ran nearly five to one against the dearest.

How they are made

A PDF is a bad shape for the internet. A page is not.

Most of the people you want to reach will read the executive summary, if that. The numbers are good. They are just locked in a file nobody opens on a phone.

01

The figures are text

Every number on the page is a real element in the HTML. Search engines index it, screen readers read it, and you can select it. The data table behind each chart is there for anyone who wants the numbers in full.

02

The charts run

Each figure is a mechanism in 3D that plays when the reader taps it. The animation is the argument: the level behind the rate, the band the headline never left, the wedge at the pump. Tap again to replay.

03

The sources are named

Every report names its dataset, the fields used, the licence and the caveats. Where a figure was computed rather than published, the page says so and shows how it was computed.
