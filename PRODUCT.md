## Product Purpose

Artifisi Sdn Bhd is an AI company with an AI division and a games division. The AI division builds Dunia, an SDK that sits inside a game engine and records state, action and outcome at each decision while someone plays. Each step comes out labelled, as training data for robots and physical AI. The games division makes original games, which are also where Dunia runs first.

Success for the site is a pilot application, from either side, through the contact form.

## Positioning

A game engine already tracks what the world looked like, what the player did and what happened next. Dunia records that as labelled data without anyone labelling by hand. Artifisi also makes its own games, so it can build a scenario for a task and record real play in it.

## Operating Context

- Exports are episodes of state, action and outcome, in the format the team trains on, for example RLDS.
- The SDK snapshots each decision, not every frame.
- A pilot for an AI team: they name the task and format, Artifisi builds a game scenario, records play, and the team tests the data in its own pipeline.
- A pilot for a studio: Dunia goes inside their game, play is recorded as labelled data, and both work on turning that data into revenue for the game.
